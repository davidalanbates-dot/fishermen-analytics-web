## Prince Court Medical Centre: Growing Search in the AI Overview Era

SEO/GEO showcase · SEO/GEO, Content, Data Analytics

**Challenge:** AI Overviews now answer health questions on the results page, so fewer people click through to hospital websites. Searches for the Prince Court name were also falling, down 11.9%, while the hospital needed to stay among the top private hospitals in the Central Region.

**What we did** (11 months)

- Planned content from trending searches and real patient questions, tied to Prince Court's priority specialties: 137 articles written, rewritten or optimised.
- Structured every page for AI answers: clear questions, direct answers, FAQ and local schema, and an llms.txt file.
- Fixed the technical foundations so search engines and AI tools can crawl, understand and cite the site.

**Results** (organic: Sep 2025 to Jul 2026 vs the year before; AI: Aug 2025 vs Jul 2026)

- +14.9% organic sessions, despite an 11.9% drop in branded searches and AI Overviews taking clicks
- +71.2% unbranded organic traffic: new patients finding Prince Court through conditions and treatments
- +442% organic article sessions and +12.3% specialty-page sessions, led by orthopaedics, O&G, paediatrics and cardiology
- +15.2% organic conversions and +12.7% returning organic visitors
- AI visibility from 0 to 47, AI search health from 60 to 82, and SEO score from 65 to 79
- +79% AI citations and +79% pages cited by AI, with AI assistants now a new source of visitors

## KWC Fashion Wholesale: Bilingual Fashion Storytelling

Finalist, Marketing Excellence Awards, Excellence in Multilingual Marketing · SEO/GEO, Content

**Challenge:** After Google rolled out AI Overviews, organic search visits to Malaysia's largest fashion wholesale mall fell 51% in six months, in a market already dominated by bigger retail hubs.

**What we did**

- Wrote fashion content in English and Bahasa Malaysia built to be picked up by AI Overviews, in a truly Malaysian voice rather than straight translation.
- Ran weekly predictive keyword research in both languages, so articles on trends, events and festive fashion went live before interest peaked.

**Results** (eight months before vs after)

- +26.5% organic search traffic, overtaking pre-AI Overview levels
- +12.8% search impressions
- From 0% to 20% of target fashion keywords featuring KWC in AI Overviews

## World Vision Malaysia: Sponsoring More Children with SEO

Winner, Marketing Excellence Awards, Excellence in Search Marketing · SEO, Data Analytics

**Challenge:** Organic traffic fell 11% while searches in the sector rose, and child sponsorships dropped 4.7%. Larger charities were outspending World Vision on paid search.

**What we did**

- Used Google Analytics to find the pages people read before donating, and optimised those first.
- Rewrote the site in two months in a conversational, question-and-answer style to win position zero, voice and AI queries.
- Kept content fresh and had each page re-crawled as soon as it went live.

**Results** (H2 2023 vs H2 2022)

- +30% organic traffic
- +66% organic search impressions
- +24% child sponsorship revenue and +7.5% children sponsored
- +83% revenue from other donation programmes

## Southern Valley (Farmbyte): Raya Confirm Ong

Winner, Marketing Excellence Awards, Excellence in Specific Audience · Creative, Video, Social, SEO

**Challenge:** A premium MD2 pineapple brand less than a year old, in a market where pineapple consumption has fallen since 2017 and rivals had a ten-year head start.

**What we did**

- Found the audience in the data: Malay–Chinese marriages are rising, and Chinese households buy pineapples for prosperity ("ong").
- Made a Hari Raya film about an inter-ethnic couple, starring Alynna Wong, with pineapples bringing ong to the celebrations.
- Placed content on popular local sites to build long-term search visibility for pineapple searches.

**Results** (campaign quarter vs the quarter before)

- +43% sales revenue
- +40% retail distribution
- +990% Instagram and +1,000% TikTok profile visits during the campaign
- Named one of the top Hari Raya videos of 2024 by Cilisos and Beautiful Nara
