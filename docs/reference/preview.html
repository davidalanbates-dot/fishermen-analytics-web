About

The data-led Fishermen.

Fishermen Analytics is an independent Malaysian agency, founded in 2014. We help brands understand their audience through data and user behaviour, then reach them with search, content and creative that performs.

How we work

Find the fish. Craft the hook. Reel in the results.

01 · Insight

Find the fish

Every audience behaves differently. We use tools like GWI, social insight platforms and your own analytics to understand exactly who we are trying to reach.

02 · Creative

Craft the hook

Ideas, content and entertainment built on that insight, so the message lands with the people it was made for.

03 · Analytics

Reel in the results

We measure, test and optimise across search, social and the web, so budget moves to what works.
